Changes / 修改 / 変更 (2026-10-02)
New static full-body extension of the approved v0.1.0 head and closed omega
smile. Reconstructed hands-on-hips pose, puff-sleeved maid dress, shaped bib,
pleated bell skirt, ruffled apron, modeled whale emblem, and one continuous
curved whale tail with broad paired flukes. Two separate stockinged legs,
Mary Jane shoes and side/back forms are newly inferred design additions.
The supplied costume/expression reference is cropped below the skirt: it
does not establish a canonical design for the legs, stockings or shoes.
That supplied reference image is not included or redistributed in this pack.

This is an AI-assisted procedural interpretation, not an original creator
model, recovered hidden detail, or a canonical turnaround. Static mesh only:
no skeleton, rig, facial blendshapes, physics or animation. No existing
MMD/Warudo mesh was copied or converted. Generated with Blender 4.3.2;
self-contained GLB uses mesh geometry, materials and vertex hair color.
