Deep Whale Maid v0.4.0 / 鲸鱼娘立体细修版 / クジラ娘立体感改良版

English
Static full-body model with a section-shaped skull and jaw, continuous facial
profile, integrated eye/lid transitions, coordinated upper lids and brows, fuller
torso/garment depth, re-seated accessories and softly curved long hair. Keeps the
C-inspired cute expression, both legs and the plump whale tail. Final refinements
include a shorter rounded chin/lower cheeks, narrower face, longer omega smile,
wider eye spacing, tilted deep-to-light blue irises, small pupils and highlights.
The source .blend retains
named separate editable mesh parts and a packed copy of the approved generated
turnaround. Open in Blender 4.3.2 or a compatible later release.
The GLB is self-contained. No rig, animation, facial morph system or physics.
Unseen views, hidden construction and footwear remain interpretive. This is not
an official model or a claim of exact reproduction of the illustration.

Read NOTICE.txt, UPSTREAM-NOTICE.txt and LICENSE-ARTWORK.txt. Artwork/model/renders
retain CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: non-commercial, complete attribution and ShareAlike.
The original uploaded reference is not included. The generated reference is a
licensed derivative design guide, not a calibrated blueprint.

The editable .blend is the authoritative final source. The optional construction
script uses the authoritative v0.3 Blend as an input; it is not needed to edit or
preview this model. The construction script records the accepted parameters; converted curve history
and floating-point sampling mean it is not an exact reconstruction of the saved scene.
Email-compatible model packages exclude executable script files.
