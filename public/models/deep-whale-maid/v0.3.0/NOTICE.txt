A reference-fidelity refinement of the v0.2 static full-body reconstruction.
New shallow, shaped eyes use painted vertex-color gradients rather than raised
circular discs. Flattened swept hair clumps have finer blue tips, a thinner looped
ahoge and softer cheek-framing locks. Headband lace, fin undersides, ribbons,
cuffs and apron edges are thinner. The closed omega smile, hands-on-hips pose,
navy maid dress, whale emblem and two stockinged legs/shoes remain.
At the user's explicit request, the curved whale tail and paired flukes are
noticeably fuller and rounder for a cuter silhouette.

The supplied costume/expression reference is cropped below the skirt: legs,
stockings, Mary Jane shoes, side/back volumes and tail depth are inferred design
additions, not canonical creator details or recovered hidden detail.
The supplied reference image is not included or redistributed in this pack.
This is an AI-assisted procedural interpretation, not an original creator model
or a canonical turnaround. Static mesh only: no skeleton, rig, facial blendshapes,
physics or animation. No existing MMD/Warudo mesh was copied or converted.
Generated in Blender 4.3.2. Self-contained GLB uses mesh geometry, materials and
vertex colors, with no external texture URL or decoder dependency.
