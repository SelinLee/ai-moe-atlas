# Rough anime digital doodles · Prompt template

[中文](DOODLE.zh.md) · [English](DOODLE.en.md) · [日本語](DOODLE.ja.md)

November 3, 2026 · Generated style adaptations of seven existing characters, each a 1254 × 1254 half-body portrait on white. Created with built-in image_gen using the front view of the current chibi sheets as the identity reference.

## Copyable template

Upload the character reference, copy the complete prompt below and replace the final {{placeholders}} with specific identity details. State viewer-left and viewer-right explicitly for different-colored eyes, side braids, hats and other asymmetric features.

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
The input image provides character identity. Draw only one half-body portrait of the front-facing character.

Convert the reference character into an extremely casual, rough, naive Japanese moe anime digital doodle, as if quickly drawn with coarse crayon and marker brushes in ordinary painting software. Preserve the recognizable hairstyle, hair color, facial features, outfit, accessories and characteristic expression.

Linework
- Use conspicuous rough black strokes with extremely uneven thickness, sudden heavy sections and breaks.
- Keep contours loose, imperfect and slightly crooked, with visible hand-drawn grain and smudging.
- Allow misalignment, overlapping lines and incomplete outlines. Avoid precise, smooth vector linework.

Color
- Use large areas of simple opaque flat color.
- Allow color to spill across outlines, missed patches and small white gaps.
- Add only a few rough gray or darker same-hue shadow strokes.
- Use casual short white strokes for highlights.
- Keep a mixed crayon, pastel and marker texture. Avoid complex gradients, meticulous lighting and many color layers.

Face
- Preserve the reference eye colors, eye shapes and characteristic expression.
- Simplify the eyes into thick black lines and basic color shapes.
- Keep the mouth simple. The nose may be omitted.
- A few loose pink blush strokes are allowed. Preserve the character’s temperament.

Hair and clothing
- Keep the most recognizable hairstyle and hair colors intact.
- Build hair from a few large color shapes without many fine strands.
- Preserve the original clothing colors, silhouette and signature accessories.
- Greatly simplify folds and decoration. Keep the reference outfit and accessories.

Composition
- Square 1:1 canvas, one centered half-body character, with a slightly oversized head.
- Leave natural white space around the subject.
- Pure white background, without added text, complex scenery or decorative frames.

The result must feel rough, scribbly, simple and naive, with low finish, thick black contours, broad flat colors, casual white highlights and clearly visible crayon grain. Avoid automatic polishing, refined line art, intricate hair structure, delicate gradients, 3D effects, excessive lighting and extra costume detail.

Keep horns, ears and headwear fully inside the frame. Use fewer strokes and just a few grouped hair shapes and essential costume marks. Express two-tone hair as separate flat-color areas. Add no title, signature, watermark or floating text.

Lock the identity to the reference
Character {{character name}}
Hair {{hairstyle, colors and color regions}}
Eyes and expression {{eye colors, viewer-left/right mapping, signature expression}}
Outfit and accessories {{original palette, key accessories and viewer-left/right placement}}
Essential identity anchors {{three to five recognizable features}}
```
